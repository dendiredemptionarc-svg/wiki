# BLOWPIE

---
**SPECIES**: Humanoid blowfish  
**APPEARANCE**: Pink/salmon colored with spiky protrusions  
**KNOWN FOR**: G*BOY's companion, Mindverse traveler  
**STATUS**: Active in Mindverse network  

---

**BLOWPIE** is a humanoid blowfish who serves as [G*BOY](G-BOY.md)'s companion and travels with him through [the Mindverse](../concepts/The-Mindverse.md). BLOWPIE is characterized by a pink/salmon colored body with spiky protrusions typical of blowfish physiology, simple facial features, and a nervous yet loyal demeanor.

**At a glance**

- Humanoid blowfish who serves as companion to [G*BOY](G-BOY.md) during Mindverse travels
- Pink/salmon colored with characteristic blowfish spiky protrusions
- Appears to have simple personality and nervous disposition
- Displaced to [Shinkai Metropolis](../locations/Shinkai-Metropolis.md) alongside G*BOY via "ritual accident"
- Escaped Shinkai with G*BOY via stolen flying vessel
- Made public appearance on [Market Talk](../organizations/Market-Talk.md) financial program

## Overview

BLOWPIE's origins remain unclear, though the humanoid blowfish appears closely bonded with [G*BOY](G-BOY.md) and accompanies him during travels through mental realms. BLOWPIE's physical form is distinctive—a pink/salmon colored blowfish-like body with characteristic spiky protrusions and simple facial features that convey emotional responses.

## Displacement to Shinkai Metropolis

BLOWPIE and [G*BOY](G-BOY.md) were displaced to [Shinkai Metropolis](../locations/Shinkai-Metropolis.md), a mind place within [the Mindverse](../concepts/The-Mindverse.md), through what sources describe as "a tear in the realities." This dimensional rift was allegedly caused by "a ritual accident"—possibly connected to [The Bloc](../organizations/The-Bloc.md)'s ritual activities.

Upon arrival in Shinkai Metropolis, BLOWPIE and G*BOY were identified as "unwanted anomalies" by local authorities. The Oni King ordered their execution, with the pair held captive in a subordinate's flying vessel pending judgment.

## Escape from Shinkai

BLOWPIE and G*BOY escaped captivity by commandeering the ornate flying ship in which they were being held. G*BOY piloted the stolen vessel despite admitting he didn't "know how to fly this thing" and being uncertain "where here is," suggesting confusion about their location within the Mindverse network.

During the escape, G*BOY explained to BLOWPIE the nature of "head jack" abilities, mind places, and the broader Mindverse network. BLOWPIE served as audience for this exposition, responding with apparent nervousness and uncertainty ("I'm not so sure...") while navigating through various mental realms.

The pair traveled through multiple mind places during their escape, including:

- European-style fairy tale villages with castle architecture
- Dark dungeons with giant creatures and ancient stone chambers
- Cyberpunk cityscapes with neon lighting
- Murky swamp or underground environments with twisted vegetation

## Relationship with G*BOY

BLOWPIE demonstrates close companionship with G*BOY, remaining alongside him through dimensional displacement, captivity, escape, and exploration of mind places. The creature appears to rely on G*BOY for navigation and understanding of their circumstances, while G*BOY treats BLOWPIE as trusted companion and explanation recipient.

BLOWPIE's simpler personality and nervous responses provide counterbalance to G*BOY's more confident, explanatory demeanor. The visual contrast between BLOWPIE's cute, simple design and the complex, often dangerous environments they navigate creates distinctive dynamic.

## Public Appearance

### Market Talk Co-hosting

In December 2025, BLOWPIE appeared as a co-host on [Market Talk](../organizations/Market-Talk.md), a financial analysis program, during coverage of the [$GBOY memecoin](G-BOY.md) launch. This appearance suggests BLOWPIE may have the ability to manifest or appear in contexts beyond the Mindverse.

During the broadcast, BLOWPIE expressed enthusiastic support for the $GBOY token, stating: "I love it. I got airdropped some -- and I staked it, and I bought more, and -- ha! I'm first when you try to --"

BLOWPIE's bullish enthusiasm contrasted sharply with the bearish analysis provided by other Market Talk panel members. Fellow co-host [Cynthia Milkins](../people/Cynthia-Milkins.md) cut off BLOWPIE mid-sentence with exasperated profanity, though the nature of BLOWPIE's interrupted statement remains unknown.

The appearance demonstrated BLOWPIE's contrarian investment approach and unwavering support for G*BOY-related projects despite negative market sentiment from financial analysts.

## Nature and Abilities

BLOWPIE's exact nature remains unclear. The humanoid blowfish may be:

- A mind place entity native to the Mindverse who adopted blowfish form
- A creation or companion manifested through G*BOY's abilities
- An experimental subject from [Saint Juniper Research Campus](../locations/Saint-Juniper-Research-Campus.md)
- An independent consciousness with blowfish physiology traveling with G*BOY

BLOWPIE's ability to appear on Market Talk suggests either:

- Capability to manifest in physical reality or media broadcasts
- The Market Talk broadcast itself exists within the Mindverse network
- BLOWPIE possesses abilities to cross between mental and physical dimensions

The humanoid blowfish form may be BLOWPIE's true appearance or a manifestation chosen for navigation through the Mindverse.
