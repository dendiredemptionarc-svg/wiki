# Agent K

---
**OCCUPATION**: High-level operative  
**ORGANIZATION**: Unknown (associated with Miter Corporation operations)  
**ACTIVE**: Early 2000s (documented)  
**STATUS**: Unknown  

---

Agent K is a high-level operative associated with [G-304](../subjects/G-304.md) recapture operations and oversight of [St. Juniper Research Campus](../locations/Saint-Juniper-Research-Campus.md) activities. The designation appears in multiple operational contexts, including remote viewing protocols, recapture missions, and a judgment narrative delivered to [Erik Purdue](Erik-Purdue.md).

**At a glance**

- High-level operative with authority over G-304 containment and recapture operations
- Commanded Nocturne Squadron in efforts to recapture G-304 after escape
- Received escalated remote viewing attempts and briefings using non-standard communications
- Delivered formal judgment to Erik Purdue regarding G-304 containment failures
- Described in witness accounts as figure in dark coat and black fedora

## Overview

Agent K operates at a level of authority above standard facility management, with access to specialized operational assets and unconventional protocols. The designation appears across multiple document types, from technical remote viewing reports to operational briefings to narrative judgment sequences.

The precise nature of Agent K's authority, organizational affiliation, and capabilities remains unclear. Evidence suggests involvement in both standard security operations and activities involving non-conventional methods.

## Documented Activities

### G-304 Recapture Operations

Following G-304's escape from St. Juniper Research Campus, Agent K assumed operational command of recapture efforts. A facility address delivered during this period stated: "Agent K and the Nocturne Squadron are moments away from putting this G*BOY issue behind us."

This indicates:

- Direct command authority over Nocturne Squadron
- Responsibility for resolving the G-304 containment breach
- Access to specialized operational units

### Remote Viewing Escalation

An internal [Miter Corporation](../organizations/Miter-Corporation.md) report on failed remote viewing attempts to locate G-304 concluded with a directive: "Escalate all future RV attempts to Agent K. Higher level alignment capability may allow target acquisition. Reader Subjects are not to continue independent attempts until further notice."

This escalation suggests Agent K possessed "higher level alignment capability" unavailable to standard remote viewing subjects, implying either:

- Enhanced psychic or perceptual abilities
- Access to superior technical methods for consciousness-based location
- Authorization to deploy assets with greater capabilities

### Admonishment of Erik Purdue

Agent K delivered a formal admonishment to Erik Purdue, then-Director of Operations at St. Juniper Research Campus, regarding the handling and eventual escape of subject G-304. The judgment accused Purdue of misusing advanced research capabilities and failing to follow proper protocols for G-304's containment and operational use.

The specific circumstances of this admonishment and the nature of Agent K's authority to deliver such judgment are not detailed in publicly available records.

## Appearance

Agent K has been described in witness accounts as wearing a dark coat and black fedora. Few verified photographs or detailed descriptions exist in accessible documentation.

## Operational Authority

Agent K's documented activities indicate authority including:

- Command of specialized operational units ([Nocturne Squadron](../organizations/Nocturne-Squadron.md))
- Access to enhanced remote viewing capabilities
- Receipt of high-level briefings using non-standard communications
- Judgment authority over facility directors
- Involvement in containment and recapture protocols for high-value subjects

The full scope of Agent K's role, organizational position, and capabilities remains undocumented in available records.
