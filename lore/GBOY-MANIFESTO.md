# G*BOY Manifesto (i-iii)

## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 1, Loot Drop 2

**Key Revelations:**
- First public document from The Bloc (1NK, Z-L1N3, GUMM1, 1999)
- Reveals Miter Corporation's research into human minds controlling reality
- Harmony drug is part of a larger "Harmony Program" for sedation and control
- The Bloc discovered this through hacking into Miter Corporation files
- Warns of "coercive homogeny" and unification of consciousness
- Miter's primary focus is mind control, not just weapons
