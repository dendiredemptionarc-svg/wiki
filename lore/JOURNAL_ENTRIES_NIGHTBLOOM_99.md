# Journal Entries - nightbloom_99

## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 2

**Key Revelations:**
- Journal entries from October 1999 - February 2000
- nightbloom_99 is best friends with starryeyed77 (uses her screen name for privacy)
- Works in IT, dating someone named Mike
- Starts taking Harmony in November 1999 - it works on them
- Journal entries become progressively shorter, less emotional, more "stable"
- By February 2000, entries are minimal: "Everything is good. No problems."
- Represents the typical Harmony response - emotional flattening disguised as peace
- Contrast to starryeyed77 who doesn't respond to Harmony at all

**Timeline Highlights:**
- October 14-28, 1999: Anxious, overthinking, self-critical entries
- November 4, 1999: Doctor recommends Harmony at annual checkup
- November 11, 1999: Picks up Harmony prescription, nervous about starting
- November 18, 1999: Week one on Harmony - notices feeling "quieter inside"
- November 27, 1999: Mother notices they seem "different" on Harmony
- December-January: Entries become increasingly brief and emotionally flat
- January 20, 2000: Has coffee with starryeyed77, notes she "seemed stressed"
- February 27, 2000: Final entry - "Everything is as it should be. No need to keep documenting when there's nothing wrong."

**Key Excerpts:**

*Before Harmony (October 21, 1999):*
> "Fixed a problem today that three other people couldn't figure out... My boss said 'good work' and I said 'oh it was nothing really' and then spent the next hour thinking about how I should have just said 'thanks' like a normal person. Why am I like this."

*Starting Harmony (November 11, 1999):*
> "Picking up my prescription at lunch. I'm nervous but I think this is the right choice. My doctor said it takes a few weeks to really feel the effects but some people notice changes sooner."

*Week one (November 18, 1999):*
> "I don't know if it's working yet or if it's placebo effect but I feel... quieter? Inside? Had a stressful thing at work (server crash right before a big presentation) and I noticed I didn't spiral about it like I normally would. Just fixed it and moved on."

*After Harmony (February 27, 2000):*
> "Been thinking about this journal lately. I started it because I was anxious all the time and needed somewhere to put all those thoughts. But I don't really feel that way anymore. Everything is stable now. Calm. Balanced. I don't think I need to keep updating like I used to. There's not much to say when everything is going well."
