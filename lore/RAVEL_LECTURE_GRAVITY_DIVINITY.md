# Ravel Lecture: Gravity and Divinity

## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 1, Loot Drop 1

**Key Revelations:**
- Dr. Ravel's philosophical lecture on consciousness and gravity
- Proposes gravity is "memory" - a trace of creation folding itself into matter
- Neural oscillations synchronize with planetary resonance (7.83 Hz / Schumann resonance) during surrender, grief, devotion, and revelation
- The "tri-axis loop" symbol represents the cycle of mind-to-matter-to-mind
- Recurring symbols across cultures exist because "the field exists, and the body need only remember it"
- "The soul is a fracture of the architect that broke itself to create this world"
- Coherent consciousness can create "openings in curvature" - seams in reality
- Emotional state determines the effect: fear = distortion, devotion = stability, awe = passage
- Ritual and geometry are ways to communicate with the source of creation
