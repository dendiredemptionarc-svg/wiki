# Miter RV Fail Report G-304

## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 1, Loot Drop 3

**Key Revelations:**
- Remote viewing (RV) attempts to locate G-304 after his escape all failed
- "Readers" (remote viewers) were unable to lock onto G-304's location
- No shared imagery across attempts - unprecedented for RV protocols
- G-304 is "not observable via standard Reader level RV methodology"
- Recommendation to escalate to "Agent K" who has "higher level alignment capability"
- Suggests G-304's escape disrupted his "reference field"
