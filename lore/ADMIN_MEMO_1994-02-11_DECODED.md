## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 1, Loot Drop 1

- The partnership was framed as funding expansion but was effectively a corporate takeover
- Introduction of stricter clearance protocols and censorship of certain terminology

From: Dr. E. Kerr — Director, Division of Gravitational Cognition
To: All Department Leads — Saint Juniper Research Campus
Subject: Administrative Notice: Transition of Oversight and Partnership with Miter Corporation
Status: Internal Use Only / Not for External Distribution

Colleagues,
After eighteen months of negotiation, Saint Juniper Research Campus will enter a formal partnership with Miter Corporation, effective March 1, 1994.
Miter will assume administrative oversight and provide expanded funding for ongoing work within the Division of Gravitational Cognition and affiliated support wings. Their stated mandate is "to advance human endurance and adaptive biology under variable conditions."

The transition requires no immediate personnel changes. Existing project charters remain valid through Q2. However, new reporting structures and data-handling procedures will be issued by Miter's Directorate of Cognitive Systems within the month. Expect much tighter clearance tiers and revised file-access protocols.

Operational Notes:
 – Laboratory communications will migrate to the sj-securelab.net internal network; credentials will be distributed by Systems Ops.
 – Future experiments must align with the joint-study directive and use standardized nomenclature provided by Miter's ethics office.

 – Miter's representatives will tour the campus next week to assess chamber integrity and observe Series B simulations. Please ensure all equipment is in compliance.

This partnership secures the continuity of our research and expands our capacity to pursue the questions that first brought us here. Treat our new colleagues with professionalism and discretion.

— Dr. E. Kerr
Division of Gravitational Cognition
Saint Juniper Research Campus
