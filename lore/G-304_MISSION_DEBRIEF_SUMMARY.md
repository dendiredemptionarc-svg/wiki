# G-304 Mission Debrief Summary

## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 1, Loot Drop 1

**Key Revelations:**
- G-304 was deployed on multiple missions with redacted external parameters
- Memory wipes were conducted after each mission ("No recall of external environment")
- Physical trauma consistently observed: bruising, fractures, thermal burns, corrosion
- Recovered anomalous items: metallic lattices, reflective glass with melted edges
- Mission 26C: G-304 and G-312 worked together and "found the edge"
- Inhibitor telemetry showed signal dropout, suggesting G-304 was breaking free of control
