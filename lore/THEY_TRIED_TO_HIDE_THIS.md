# They Tried To Hide This

## Context & Analysis

**Puzzle Origin:** Episode 1, Loot Drop 3

**Key Revelations:**
- Bloc-released version of G-304's escape surveillance log
- Confirms neural activity flatlined for two minutes before escaping
- All monitoring instruments "saturated above their measurable ceiling"
- Door records cycled between states without physical contact
- Gravity varied outside safe tolerances
- Event originally called "ascension event" before being renamed
- No verifiable visual record of the departure
